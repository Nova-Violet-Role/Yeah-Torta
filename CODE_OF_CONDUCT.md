## 📜 The spirit of this place

Nova-Violet Role is a non-profit built on a simple idea: **complex problems deserve
more than one lens.** That applies to people as much as to code. Someone arriving
with a question you find obvious is bringing a lens you have stopped using — and
that is worth having.

So the whole of it, in one line:

> **Be kind. Be curious. Bring evidence.**

Everything below is just that sentence, explained.

---

## 🌟 What we love to see

| | |
|:--|:--|
| 💡 **Questions** | There are no beginner questions here, only questions. Asking one helps the next person who was afraid to. |
| 🔬 **Evidence** | "I think this is wrong, here is the command and its output" is the most useful sentence in the repository. |
| 🎉 **Corrections** | Finding a mistake is a gift to everyone. It is celebrated, not tolerated. |
| 🌍 **Different backgrounds** | Law, design, networking, linguistics, none of the above — every lens sees something the others cannot. |
| ❤️ **Patience** | People read less than you think and more than you fear. Ask before assuming. |

---

## 🎁 Why corrections are celebrated here

This project keeps a written record of its own mistakes, and it is a long one: a
release library built without a required feature flag that silently disabled a
whole pillar; a browser page that looked perfectly protected while its DNS had
quietly gone elsewhere; a feature that, once switched on, blocked the very traffic
it was meant to accelerate.

**Every one of them looked green.** They were found by measuring instead of
assuming, and they were written down instead of hidden.

That is why nobody here loses face for being wrong — being wrong *in public, with
the output attached* is precisely how this thing gets better. If you catch the next
one, you have done the most valuable work available in this repository, and you will
be credited for it.

And if someone asks *"which instrument told you that?"* — it is never an accusation.
It is the question this whole project is organised around, and we ask it of ourselves
first.

---

## 🌱 A gentle ask

- Critique the **claim**, never the person behind it.
- Nothing about anyone's identity, background or experience level belongs in a
  technical thread.
- One person disagreeing is a conversation; five people piling on is not.
- Take special care with **privacy claims**. People may rely on this software to
  protect something that matters to them, so overstating what it does is a kindness
  to no one. Hold the marketing to the same standard as the code — and that goes for
  the maintainers most of all.

---

## 🕊️ If something goes wrong

Most friction is a misunderstanding, and a short private message usually settles it.
If it does not, reach the maintainers through a private security advisory or a direct
issue, and it will be handled confidentially and proportionately. The aim is always
to keep someone in the community rather than to remove them — and where that is not
possible, the priority is protecting the people already here.
